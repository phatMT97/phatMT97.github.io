About

For the last two years I have shipped AI and LLM products as a business analyst — document-processing and knowledge-base agents for Japanese clients — owning requirement elicitation, functional specs, UI/UX design and client demos. Before that, five years as a mainframe developer in life and non-life insurance gave me rare depth in regulated domains and legacy systems: policy lifecycle, claims and batch.

I am a COBOL and mainframe developer with five years on production insurance systems — four on IBM z/OS (COBOL, DB2, CICS, JCL) for a French non-life insurer, plus a year on the Ingenium life-insurance core. I am known for reverse-engineering undocumented legacy code to deliver new functionality, and I now apply that depth on AI projects that document and modernise legacy COBOL.
